How to get your Transcript PDF
  1. Log in to your school's grade portal on a computer.
  2. Navigate to the Grades or Transcript page.
  3. Right-click anywhere on the page and select Print.
  4. Change the Destination/Printer to "Save as PDF".
  5. Click Save, then upload that file here!
